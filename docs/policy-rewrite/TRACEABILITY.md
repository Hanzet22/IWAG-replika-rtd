## Legal and policy review framework

The rewrite is designed to be checked against the following legal families before any event operator adopts it:

- **Indonesia:** Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection; Government Regulation No. 17 of 2025 on governance of electronic systems in child protection; Law No. 8 of 1999 on Consumer Protection; Law No. 8 of 2016 on Persons with Disabilities; Law No. 12 of 2022 on Sexual Violence Crimes; child-protection legislation; electronic-system and venue-safety requirements.
- **Conditional cross-border privacy:** GDPR and ePrivacy rules for processing within their territorial scope; UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018; US COPPA where a covered online service is directed to or knowingly collects from children under 13; Singapore PDPA; Malaysia PDPA. These are not automatically applicable merely because an international attendee reads a page.
- **Operational standards:** age-appropriate safeguarding, data minimisation, least-privilege access, accessible communications, incident reporting, fair notice and review, proportionate sanctions, vendor due diligence and clear consumer disclosures.

The applicable law depends on the actual contracting entity, controller/processor roles, venue, service design, data flows, target audience and attendee locations. The final operator must verify the current text, commencement, territorial reach and any implementing rules before adopting a clause. This matrix deliberately distinguishes mandatory legal duties from event-policy choices and recommended safeguards; it does not treat every recommended practice as a statutory obligation.

## Material V2 improvements

1. Blanket no-refund and blanket liability language is replaced with event-specific cancellation scenarios and preservation of mandatory consumer and liability rights.
2. Unilateral cancellation, badge revocation and permanent exclusion are replaced with documented grounds, evidence assessment, notice, opportunity to respond and review, subject to urgent safety exceptions.
3. A criminal investigation, allegation, lawful expression or another event's ban is not treated as conclusive proof of harmful conduct.
4. Identity-document collection is limited by purpose, proportionality, access restriction and deletion.
5. Photography and publicity are separated into crowd context, identifiable close-ups, press/production and restricted camera-free spaces.
6. Minors' participation and guardian verification are treated as safeguarding and data-protection matters, not merely waiver collection.
7. Creative submissions retain creator ownership unless a separate written transfer exists; event-use permissions are limited to disclosed purposes.
8. English and Indonesian versions contain equivalent rule coverage and procedural safeguards.

## Known verification items before adoption

- Confirm the legal identity and authority of the event organiser and contracting party.
- Confirm the current event edition's age table, guardian policy, pass tiers, payment recipient, transfer deadlines and refund schedule.
- Confirm the current venue's written restrictions, accessible facilities, emergency plan, food rules, pool rules and media permissions.
- Confirm actual data fields, controller/processor roles, payment-provider return fields, hosting regions, vendor contracts, retention periods and incident contacts.
- Confirm official reporting contacts, safeguarding lead, complaint escalation and appeal decision-maker.
- Confirm rights and licences for event marks, mascots, artwork, photographs, fonts and any source-derived media.

The absence of a verified operational fact is recorded as a verification item; it is not replaced with an invented address, contact, price, deadline, provider or legal identity.
