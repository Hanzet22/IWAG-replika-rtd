These are facts displayed by the original site at the time of this review, not independently verified contracts or guarantees. Any V2 page must identify the original source and last-checked date, then recheck volatile details before publication. Do not copy countdown values, room prices, schedules, ticket availability, or travel claims without a fresh check.

## 3. Important policy observations (not adopted as V2 policy)

- The Code of Conduct states a 14+ event content rating, parental consent for attendees aged 14–16, and guardian accompaniment for children under 14. This is the event's published policy, not proof that it alone satisfies Indonesian child-data or child-online-safety obligations.
- The Code covers harassment, badge display, public-area conduct, smoking/vaping, photography consent, fursuit-lounge camera restrictions, prop checks, controlled substances, hotel rules and staff/security directions.
- The Terms page describes event passes, payment, transfers, no-show and cancellation. Its non-refundable language must not be copied as a default clause or treated as automatically enforceable.
- The Privacy page refers to Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law. V2 must assess the actual data flow, legal basis, controller/processor roles, vendors, retention and data-subject rights; a policy statement alone does not establish compliance.
- The Terms page describes staff as a non-corporate social organization in Jakarta. That wording is not a substitute for documentary verification of the actual contracting party, legal operator, data controller, registered business or event permit holder.

## 4. Rights, attribution and independent-project boundary

The original Brand Guideline states that third parties should consult IWAG and obtain approval before using the IWAG logo in collaborative projects. It states that Kiki and Tama are offered under a Creative Commons BY-NC licence and credits Reo for the reference sheets.

Implementation rules:

1. Do not copy IWAG's logo, mascots, photographs, illustrations, maps, staff portraits, video, audio, fonts or promotional assets into this repository without a documented rights basis.
2. For CC BY-NC material, confirm that the actual asset is covered by that licence, provide the required attribution and licence link, identify modifications, and do not use it commercially without separate permission.
3. A website that promotes paid tickets, merchandise or sponsorship may have commercial context. Do not assume use is non-commercial.
4. Record source, creator, owner, licence, permission, permitted edits, credit text and expiry/review date in an asset register.
5. Do not scrape or republish third-party travel-blog text, maps or photographs just because the original guide links to them.
6. Keep the independent-project notice visible. Do not use official-sounding labels, contact addresses or branding that could mislead visitors about affiliation.

## 5. Content migration rules

- Preserve route coverage where useful, but do not preserve inaccurate content.
- Label content as **current and source-verified**, **historical**, **awaiting verification**, or **not published**.
- Record a source URL and `lastReviewed` date for volatile facts (venue, date, rates, transport, visa, payment methods, ticketing and sponsor benefits).
- Use primary sources for immigration, customs, transport, venue accessibility, public safety and consumer transactions.
- Do not publish the names/contact details of staff from the source site in this independent project without a clear purpose and rights/privacy review.
- Never invent operator identity, event schedule, ticket price, refund promise, sponsor, contact channel or accessibility service.
- Legal policy pages remain clearly marked as drafts until the real operator, controller, services and legal reviewer are confirmed.
