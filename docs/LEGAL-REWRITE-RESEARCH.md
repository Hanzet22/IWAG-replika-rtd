# IWAG V2 — Legal Rewrite Research & Jurisdictional Applicability

**As of:** 23 November 2026. **Purpose:** research for nonbinding drafts, not legal advice.

## Method and known service facts
The application is a static informational preview without attendee accounts, registration, ticketing, payments, contact forms, public comments, user uploads or intentional analytics. It stores theme and accent preferences in browser local storage. Hosting, CDN, DNS, security logs, provider cookies, deployment telemetry, operator identity and retention settings are unverified. Global accessibility alone does not automatically trigger every country's laws; assess establishment, territorial processing, targeted offering, monitoring, commercial activity, child access and service classification.

## Indonesia — baseline
- **UU 27/2022 Pelindungan Data Pribadi:** assess controller/processor roles, lawful basis, transparency, purpose limitation, rights, security, retention, breach response, child data and cross-border transfers. Include infrastructure logs if processed. https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022
- **PP 71/2019** and **UU 1/2024** amending the ITE Law: assess electronic-system classification, security, information duties and private PSE registration triggers against actual operator/functions. https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/122030/pp-no-71-tahun-2019 ; https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/274494/uu-no-1-tahun-2024
- **PP 17/2025** and **Permenkomdigi 9/2026:** assess whether products/services/features are designed for or may be accessed by children and applicable risk controls. An event admission age does not settle website applicability. https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/316698/pp-no-17-tahun-2025 ; https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/346040/permenkomdigi-no-9-tahun-2026
- **UU 8/1999** and **PP 80/2019:** assess if commercial supply, consumer offers, passes/merchandise or payment are introduced. https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/45288/uu-no-8-tahun-1999 ; https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/126143/pp-no-80-tahun-2019
- **UU 28/2014 Hak Cipta:** review text, photos, illustrations, logos, fonts, video, music, maps, code, licences, attribution and moral rights. Attribution alone does not authorise copying. https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/38690/uu-no-28-tahun-2014
- **UU 8/2016 Penyandang Disabilitas:** informs equal-access obligations; exact duties for this private site require classification and local review. https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/37251/uu-no-8-tahun-2016
- A prior research note identified **PP 33/2026** with a stated 16 January 2027 commencement, but official gazette/BPK text must be independently verified before relying on article-level requirements or claiming it is in force.

## EU / EEA
- **GDPR 2016/679:** Article 3 scope depends on EU establishment or offering goods/services to people in the EU or monitoring behaviour; mere accessibility is not the full test. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- **ePrivacy 2002/58/EC:** assess cookies and device storage/access under national implementing laws; non-essential technologies generally require consent subject to narrow exemptions. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj
- **Digital Services Act 2022/2065:** reassess if comments, uploads, profiles, listings or messaging are introduced. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj
- **European Accessibility Act 2019/882:** assess listed services, including e-commerce if ticketing/booking is added. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj

## United Kingdom
- **UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018:** assess establishment or targeting/monitoring; global accessibility alone is insufficient. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/contents
- **PECR:** inspect cookies and similar technologies. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/
- **Age Appropriate Design Code:** assess likely child access and personal-data processing. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/

## Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand
- **Singapore PDPA:** assess organisational collection/use/disclosure and notice, purpose, protection, retention, transfer and breach duties. https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/personal-data-protection-act
- **Malaysia PDPA 2010 Act 709:** assess commercial-transaction and territorial/equipment triggers; noncommercial access alone does not establish applicability. https://www.pdp.gov.my/
- **Thailand PDPA 2019:** assess establishment and extraterritorial targeting/monitoring. https://www.mdes.go.th/law/detail/3577-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-B-E--2562--2019-

## United States
- **COPPA:** assess commercial status, child-directed factors, actual knowledge of collection from under-13 users, and third-party collection via cookies/embeds/identifiers. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/childrens-privacy
- **CCPA/CPRA and state laws:** assess business status, California nexus, thresholds, sensitive data, sale/sharing, profiling and exemptions. https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/
- **FTC Act:** privacy, security, sponsorship and service claims must not be deceptive. https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/statutes/federal-trade-commission-act

## Nine policy requirements
1. **CoC:** controlled-space scope, conduct, harassment/threats/doxxing, reporting, proportionate action, fairness and appeal; no promises of nonexistent staffing.
2. **Terms:** verified parties, actual scope, acceptable use, age, IP, links, availability, consumer rights, changes, governing law and disputes only after review.
3. **Privacy:** controller, real data sources, purposes/bases, vendors, transfers, retention, rights, children, security and complaints.
4. **Cookie:** actual cookies/device storage, provider, purpose, duration, consent and controls; no cookie-free claim before production inspection.
5. **Copyright/IP:** rights, licences, attribution, trademarks, notices and any future user-submission licence.
6. **Disclaimer:** independence, dated information, external links, professional-advice limits and non-excludable rights.
7. **Accessibility:** scope, target, audit date/method, barriers, alternatives, remediation and contact; no premature conformance claim.
8. **Moderation/reporting:** current absence of user content; future intake, triage, evidence, action, appeals and child-safety escalation.
9. **Retention:** inventory, purpose, owner, vendor, location, trigger, duration, deletion, backups, legal holds and rights requests.

## Unverified facts
Do not invent operator/controller, legal form, address, contact, providers, cookies, logs, data locations, retention periods, backup rotation, moderation staff, response deadlines, business status, revenue, ticketing, age distribution or asset permissions. Reconcile drafts with deployed behaviour and vendor contracts. Indonesian counsel should review the baseline; local advice is needed where another jurisdiction is deliberately targeted or materially triggered.

**Conclusion:** research-backed drafting only; not a guarantee of worldwide compliance and not legal advice.


## Additional international jurisdictions — research addendum

This is a jurisdictional screening list, not a claim that every listed law applies. Territorial tests and current amendments must be checked against the operator, targeting, actual processing, vendors and users.

### Canada
- **PIPEDA** applies to personal information handled in commercial activities and certain interprovincial/international flows; a genuinely noncommercial static publication is not automatically covered. Assess federal/provincial allocation and whether activity is commercial. Official statute (current consolidation): https://lois-laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/
- **Quebec private-sector privacy law (Law 25 amendments)** applies to enterprises handling personal information in Quebec, subject to its territorial and commercial context. Reassess if the project markets or contracts in Quebec or uses local vendors. https://legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/showdoc/cs/p-39.1

### Australia
- **Privacy Act 1988** has an Australian-link test for overseas conduct and small-business exemptions with exceptions; assess local business, turnover and collection facts rather than mere reachability. https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03712/2026-06-04/2026-06-04/text
- **Online Safety Act 2021** and the social-media minimum-age regime regulate specified service categories; a read-only publisher without user interaction is not automatically an age-restricted social-media platform. https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2024A00127/asmade/2024-12-10/text
- Australian Human Rights Commission guidance treats web-delivered services as potentially covered by disability discrimination law; exact territorial nexus for an Indonesian-operated site requires advice. https://humanrights.gov.au/resource-hub/by-resource-type/publications/guidelines/articles/disability-rights/disability-rights-and-the-disability-discrimination-act/world-wide-web-access-disability-discrimination-act-advisory-notes-ver

### Japan
- **APPI** may reach an overseas business processing information about people in Japan in connection with supplying goods or services to them. Mere untargeted accessibility is a weaker trigger. Assess identifiers and recipient combination rules. https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/4241/en
- The Personal Information Protection Commission publishes current law and guidance; check the 2 November 2026 consolidated amendment materials before publication. https://www.ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/legal/

### South Korea
- **Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA)** can apply to overseas processing that substantially affects Korean data subjects; examine Korea-directed services, establishment, local contracts and identifiable identifiers. Under-14 data generally requires verifiable legal-representative consent. Official law search: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/eng/engLsSc.do?menuId=2&query=PERSONAL+INFORMATION+PROTECTION+ACT

### Brazil
- **LGPD (Law 13,709/2018)** has territorial and targeting/collection triggers under Article 3; processing of personal data collected in Brazil can be in scope even if the operator is abroad. Child/adolescent data has best-interest safeguards. ANPD cookie guidance applies ordinary LGPD principles to cookies and identifiers. https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/outros-documentos-e-publicacoes-institucionais/lgpd-en-lei-no-13-709-capa.pdf

### Philippines
- **Data Privacy Act 2012 (Republic Act 10173)** has extraterritorial provisions tied to Philippine citizens/residents plus statutory Philippine connections; do not treat mere global access as sufficient. NPC child-transparency guidance is relevant if children are likely users and personal data is processed. https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act

### New Zealand
- **Privacy Act 2020, section 4** can cover an overseas agency carrying on business in New Zealand, even without a physical office; mere passive access is not necessarily carrying on business. The Act protects personal information of all ages. https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2020/31/en/latest/

### South Africa
- **POPIA (Act 4 of 2013)** principally applies where the responsible party is domiciled in South Africa or, if not, uses processing means in South Africa other than mere transit. Child data has additional restrictions. Verify host/CDN/server locations. https://www.gov.za/documents/protection-personal-information-act

### India
- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023** includes an overseas-offering trigger for services offered to data principals in India. Commencement is phased: as of 23 November 2026, the core processing, notice/consent, security and child-data provisions were reported to commence on 13 May 2027, not yet in force on this review date. Verify the official commencement notifications and Rules before publication. https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ ; https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa

### United Arab Emirates
- **Federal PDPL** covers UAE processing and certain processing by controllers/processors inside or outside the UAE concerning people in the UAE, subject to exclusions. Verify the current executive regulations and implementing status before relying on detailed obligations. https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws
- **Federal Decree-Law No. 26 of 2025 on Child Digital Safety** took effect 1 January 2026 and requires separate scope analysis for internet providers and digital platforms operating in or directed at UAE users where children can access them. https://www.uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/3912

### Saudi Arabia
- **Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)** applies to processing in the Kingdom and to certain processing outside it concerning individuals residing in the Kingdom. Its transition period has expired; verify the current SDAIA regulations and transfer rules. https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/about/Documents/PersonalDataProtectionLaw.pdf

## Screening outcome
The additional regimes materially broaden the legal review but do not create a universal global policy. The project should maintain a legal register with jurisdiction, instrument, issuing authority, official URL, current status, effective date, territorial trigger, affected feature, evidence, reviewer and next review date. Add further jurisdictions when the operator deliberately targets them, processes identifiable local data, establishes operations there, or uses local providers.
